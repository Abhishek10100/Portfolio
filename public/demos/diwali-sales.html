Key findings

  • 11,251 transaction records analyzed; average order amount ₹9,453.6 (max ₹23,952).
  • The majority of buyers are female, and women show higher purchasing power on average than men.
  • The top buying age group is 26–35 years old; married women are the largest high-spend segment.
  • Uttar Pradesh, Maharashtra, and Karnataka lead by order volume and total sales; by occupation, IT, Healthcare, and Aviation sectors spend the most.
  • Food, Clothing & Apparel, and Electronics & Gadgets are the top revenue-generating product categories.
