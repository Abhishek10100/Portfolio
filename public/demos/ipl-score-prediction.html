Key findings

  • Best Ridge regularization strength found via cross-validation: alpha=40 (CV score -328.4 MSE).
  • Best Lasso regularization strength: alpha=1 (CV score -320.8 MSE).
  • On the held-out test set, Ridge scored MAE 12.12 / RMSE 15.84 (reproduced exactly by the live model above).
  • Lasso scored MAE 12.21 / RMSE 16.20 — Ridge edges it out slightly on every final metric.
  • Practical read: predictions are typically within about 12–16 runs of the actual first-innings total, useful as a live in-match estimate rather than an exact forecast.
