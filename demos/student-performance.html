Student Performance Indicator

A Flask web app that predicts a student's math exam score from demographic and academic inputs (gender, ethnicity, parental education, lunch type, test prep, reading/writing scores), backed by a trained regression pipeline. Unlike the other projects here, this one is a real containerized, deployed app you can actually use.

Key findings

  • 1,000 student records, 19 features after encoding demographic/categorical fields; 800/200 train/test split.
  • Benchmarked 9 regression algorithms; Ridge Regression was the best performer at R² 0.8806 on the test set, with plain Linear Regression close behind at 0.8803.
  • Weakest performer was a single Decision Tree at R² 0.7474 on test despite a near-perfect R² 0.9997 on training — a clear overfitting case that the ensemble/regularized models avoided.
  • Other results for reference: Random Forest 0.851, CatBoost 0.852, AdaBoost 0.848, XGBoost 0.828, Lasso 0.825, KNN 0.784.
  • EDA on the same data: average scores were math 66.09, reading 69.17, writing 68.05 — students in this dataset consistently performed worst in Math; students with a completed test-prep course and standard (vs free/reduced) lunch scored higher on average.
